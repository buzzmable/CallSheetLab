What this is

A game-day call sheet builder for College Football 27, built on my Alabama-style offensive playbook: 724 plays across 58 sets, inspired by the offense Kalen DeBoer and OC Ryan Grubb run at Alabama in 2026. Mostly 11 personnel with plenty of 12, a balanced run game, and play-action and RPOs built off it.

Why I made it

I wanted to call games like a real play-caller instead of scrolling the play menu between snaps. It's laid out like a real sideline sheet: a scripted opening drive, a base menu by personnel, and boxes for 3rd down, red zone, goal line and the clock, built around the defense you're actually facing.

How it works

Generate a game plan, pick the opponent's defensive playbook, and you get a full sheet. Re-roll or edit any call, and check the shift chart for every set you can shift between. Your sheets are private to you; the playbook stays the same for everyone.
